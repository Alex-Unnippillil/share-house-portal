# Acceptable Use Policy

_Last Updated: February 18, 2026_

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to all Roomsily users, including tenants, roommates, property managers, and admins.

## 1. Allowed use

You may use Roomsily only for legitimate housing operations, including rent payments, documents, amenity reservations, visitor submissions, and community communication.

## 2. Prohibited behaviors

You must not:

- submit false identity, payment, visitor, or lease information,
- bypass or attempt to bypass role-based access controls,
- misuse exports, audit logs, or tenant data outside authorized operations,
- post harassing, threatening, discriminatory, or unlawful content,
- upload malware, phishing links, or harmful scripts,
- probe, exploit, reverse engineer, or disrupt platform security.

## 3. Visitor and booking integrity

Users must provide accurate visitor and reservation details. Repeated abuse of policy-limited workflows (for example serial resubmission after policy denial) may result in account restrictions and manager escalation.

## 4. Payment and document integrity

Do not attempt fraudulent charge activity, forged receipts, or unauthorized document tampering. Financial and document events may be audited and shared with authorized property personnel.

## 5. Notification and messaging standards

Use communication channels for relevant tenancy operations. Spam, impersonation, and abusive behavior are prohibited.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations may lead to warning, suspension, feature restrictions, export revocation, or account termination. Severe incidents may be escalated to legal or regulatory authorities where required.
