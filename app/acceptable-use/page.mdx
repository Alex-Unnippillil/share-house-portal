# Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: February 18, 2026_

This policy applies to all Roomsily users, including tenants, roommates, property managers, and admins.

## 1) Permitted use

Use Roomsily only for legitimate shared-housing operations, including payments, booking requests, documents, visitor workflows, and approved communications.

## 2) Prohibited use

Do not:

- submit false identity, payment, visitor, or lease information,
- bypass role-based access controls,
- export or redistribute tenant data without authorization

  ,
- post abusive, harassing, discriminatory, or unlawful content,
- attempt malware delivery, phishing, exploitation, or disruption.

## 3) Data and record integrity

Payment records, visitor logs, document events, and audit records must remain accurate and untampered. Misrepresentation or tampering may lead to immediate restrictions.

## 4) Enforcement actions

Depending on severity, violations may result in warnings, feature restrictions, suspension, termination, export revocation, and escalation to legal/regulatory authorities.
