# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: February 18, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Roomsily collects, uses, shares, and retains personal information in tenant and manager workflows.

## At a glance

- We process data to operate housing workflows (payments, documents, bookings, messaging, and visitor logs).
- We use service providers such as Supabase, Stripe, Cal.com, Documenso, and (optionally) Plaid.
- We do not sell personal data.
- Retention windows and operational deletion jobs are described in our [Data Retention Policy](/data-retention).

## 1) Data we collect

- **Account and profile data:** name, email, role, unit, rent-share, and related tenancy details.
- **Payment metadata:** customer/payment identifiers, statuses, receipts, and reconciliation events.
- **Document metadata:** versions, envelope/signature state, and access events.
- **Booking and visitor records:** requests, approvals/rejections, and policy-evaluation outcomes.
- **Security/operations data:** authentication events, audit records, and service diagnostics.

## 2) Why we process data

We process data to:

1. provide and secure core services,
2. enforce tenancy and platform policy workflows,
3. support billing, receipts, and reconciliation,
4. meet legal, security, and recordkeeping obligations.

## 3) Legal bases and consent

Depending on region, legal bases may include contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligations, and consent.

- **Payments:** contract performance and user-initiated authorization.
- **Documents:** contractual/legal necessity for lease and compliance operations.
- **Notifications:** service necessity for critical events; optional preferences when available.
- **Optional bank linking:** explicit opt-in consent before account linking.

## 4) Service providers and sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the service and comply with law:

- Supabase (data, auth, storage, realtime)
- Stripe (payments)
- Cal.com (amenity scheduling)
- Documenso (document workflows)
- Plaid (optional bank linking)

We may also disclose data when required by legal process or to protect users and platform security.

## 5) Retention and deletion

Retention targets differ by data type. For currently enforced operational windows and audit/export commitments, see [Data Retention Policy](/data-retention).

Certain records may be retained longer when legal holds, disputes, fraud reviews, or statutory requirements apply.

## 6) Regional compliance

Regional requirements can vary for notice, retention, and housing operations. Property managers are responsible for configuring policy behavior that aligns with local law.

## 7) Your rights

Subject to local law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or objection/restriction. Submit requests through your property support or designated compliance channel.

## 8) Security practices

Roomsily applies role-based access controls, encrypted transport, authenticated access, and auditable operations for privileged workflows.

## 9) Policy updates

We may update this policy as products, integrations, or legal obligations evolve. Material changes will be communicated through in-app notices or equivalent channels.
