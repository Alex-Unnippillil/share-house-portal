# Privacy Policy

_Last Updated: February 18, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Roomsily collects, uses, shares, and retains personal data when tenants, roommates, property managers, and admins use the Roomsily portal.

## 1. Data controller context

Roomsily provides software infrastructure for properties. Depending on your region and contract structure, your property manager/landlord may act as the primary controller for tenancy records, while Roomsily operates as a processor/service provider for platform operations.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account and profile data

- Name, email, role, unit assignment, rent share, emergency contacts, and optional vehicle details.

### Financial and payment data

- Stripe customer/payment identifiers, payment status events, receipts, and reconciliation metadata.
- For optional ACH flows, tokenized bank-linking metadata (for example institution name and masked account details).

### Documents and signature data

- Uploaded files, lease versions, signature workflow identifiers, and access logs.

### Bookings, visitors, and communications

- Amenity bookings, visitor-log submissions, approvals/rejections, and in-app message/notification records.

### Security and telemetry

- Authentication events, IP/session metadata, audit records, and service diagnostics.

## 3. How we use data

We process data to:

- deliver tenant and manager workflows,
- enforce policy rules (for example visitor-night limits and booking conflicts),
- process payments and maintain receipts/reconciliation,
- send operational and compliance notifications,
- protect accounts, investigate misuse, and satisfy legal obligations.

## 4. Consent disclosures and legal bases

Depending on location, our legal bases may include contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligations, and consent.

- **Payments:** consent and contractual necessity for processing rent and related payment events.
- **Documents:** contractual/legal necessity for lease administration and compliance logging.
- **Notifications:** operational necessity for service-critical updates; consent or preference-based controls for optional communications.
- **Optional bank linking:** explicit opt-in consent before ACH account linking.

You may withdraw optional consents (for example bank-linking usage) where technically and legally available, but required tenancy/payment processing may continue under contract or legal obligations.

## 5. Sharing and subprocessors

We do not sell personal data. We share data with service providers strictly to deliver the Service, including:

- **Supabase** (database/auth/storage/realtime)
- **Stripe** (payments)
- **Cal.com** (amenity scheduling)
- **Documenso** (document workflows)
- **Plaid** (optional bank linking where enabled)

We may also disclose data when required by law, legal process, or safety/security investigations.

## 6. International transfers

Data may be processed in regions outside your residence. Where required, we use contractual and organizational safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 7. Retention schedule overview

Detailed retention commitments appear in our [Data Retention Policy](/data-retention). In summary:

- visitor logs are retained per policy/legal schedule,
- notifications are retained for operational windows,
- audit logs and export records are retained for oversight and compliance,
- documents and payment records are retained according to contractual/legal requirements.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to local law, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. Tenants should submit requests through property support channels; managers/admins should use designated operations workflows.

## 9. Security

Roomsily uses layered security controls, including authenticated access, role-based authorization, encryption in transit, and auditable administrative actions.

## 10. Region-specific requirements

Local requirements may differ for retention periods, notice obligations, and visitor-log handling.

Property managers are responsible for configuring policy settings and operational practices for their jurisdiction. Roomsily provides enforcement controls, audit trails, and exports to support compliance execution.

## 11. Children

The Service is intended for housing operations and is not directed to children under 13.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy as features, legal requirements, or integrations evolve. Material changes will be communicated through in-app notices or other reasonable channels.
