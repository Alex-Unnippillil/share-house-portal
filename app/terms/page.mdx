# Terms of Service

_Last updated: February 18, 2026_

Roomsily provides software for shared-housing operations. These Terms apply to tenant, roommate, property manager, and admin use of the Roomsily portal.

## At a glance

- Role-based access controls define what each user can view or change.
- Payments are processed by Stripe, and optional ACH linking may use Plaid.
- Documents, approvals, and key operational actions are audit logged.
- Visitor requests are policy-checked before approval workflows run.

## 1) Who can use Roomsily

You must be authorized by your property, provide accurate information, and keep your account credentials secure. You may only use features and data your role permits.

## 2) Role responsibilities

- **Tenants and roommates** are responsible for accurate profile, payment, booking, and visitor information.
- **Property managers** are responsible for policy configuration and lawful operations for their region.
- **Admins** are responsible for platform oversight and controlled operational exports.

## 3) Payment terms and consent

By initiating checkout, enabling autopay, or linking a bank account, you authorize the requested payment flow and related processing.

- Stripe handles card and billing workflows.
- Optional ACH bank linking uses tokenized flows (no raw account credentials stored by Roomsily).
- Payment receipts and status events are retained for tenant and manager reconciliation.

## 4) Documents and e-signature flows

Roomsily supports document upload, retrieval, and third-party signing workflows (for example Documenso integrations). You are responsible for reviewing agreement content before signing.

Document actions (view/download/sign/export where applicable) may be logged for legal and compliance traceability.

## 5) Notifications and operational messages

You consent to service-critical notices, such as payment failures, booking conflicts, moderation actions, and visitor approvals/rejections. Non-critical messaging preferences may be configurable when supported.

## 6) Visitor policy enforcement

Visitor requests are checked against configured policy constraints, including maximum consecutive nights, blackout windows, overlap rules, and approval requirements.

Repeated misuse, inaccurate submissions, or attempts to bypass policy checks may result in restrictions or suspension.

## 7) Acceptable use

You must not:

- use the service for unlawful, abusive, discriminatory, or fraudulent purposes,
- attempt unauthorized access or data extraction,
- upload malicious code or tamper with records,
- misuse exports or audit logs outside authorized housing operations.

See the [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use) for details.

## 8) Retention, audit, and exports

Operational data is retained under our [Data Retention Policy](/data-retention) and applicable law. Authorized roles may export supported datasets, and export activity is logged.

## 9) Regional compliance and legal scope

Property managers must align policy settings and operational practices with local tenancy, privacy, and recordkeeping obligations.

Roomsily provides configurable controls and audit tooling but does not provide legal advice.

## 10) Changes, suspension, and termination

We may update features, policies, and integrations over time. We may suspend or terminate access for policy violations, security risks, or legal obligations.

## 11) Contact

For account or legal-policy questions, contact your property management team first, then your configured Roomsily support channel.
