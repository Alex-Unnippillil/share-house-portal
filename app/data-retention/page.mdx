# Data Retention Policy

_Last updated: February 18, 2026_

This policy defines how long key operational records are retained in Roomsily and how scheduled cleanup is applied.

## Retention schedule

| Data type                                  | Baseline retention window  | Current handling                                           |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visitor logs                               | 180 days                   | Older visitor records are eligible for scheduled deletion. |
| Notifications                              | 90 days                    | Older notifications are eligible for scheduled deletion.   |
| Audit logs (including export audit events) | 730 days (24 months)       | Older audit records are eligible for scheduled deletion.   |
| Payment and document evidence              | Contractual / legal period | Retained according to legal and operational obligations.   |

## 1) Why retention exists

Retention supports:

- resident and manager operational continuity,
- policy and security investigations,
- dispute handling,
- legal and regulatory obligations.

## 2) Export traceability and compliance

Authorized operational exports (finance, maintenance, bookings, visitors) are audit logged to preserve who exported data and when.

## 3) Region-specific requirements

These are baseline platform windows. Where local law requires different retention periods, legal holds, or additional notices, property teams must apply region-appropriate process controls.

## 4) Exceptions

Data may be preserved beyond standard windows for litigation holds, active investigations, regulator requests, or unresolved disputes.
