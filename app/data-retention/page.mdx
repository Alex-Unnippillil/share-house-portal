# Data Retention Policy

_Last Updated: February 18, 2026_

This policy explains how long Roomsily retains key records used across tenant and manager portals.

## 1. Purpose

We retain data only for operational continuity, legal compliance, dispute handling, and security oversight. Retention windows may be extended when litigation holds, legal requests, or unresolved investigations apply.

## 2. Retention commitments

| Data category                               | Typical retention                      | Notes                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visitor logs                                | 180 days minimum                       | May be retained longer where local housing/security rules require it.         |
| Notification events                         | 90 days minimum                        | Supports delivery troubleshooting and operational audits.                     |
| Audit logs (admin/manager actions, exports) | 24 months minimum                      | Supports compliance verification, incident response, and access reviews.      |
| Export activity records                     | 24 months minimum                      | Tracks who exported regulated operational data and when.                      |
| Payment events and receipts                 | Per contractual/legal accounting rules | Usually tied to financial recordkeeping periods in applicable jurisdictions.  |
| Document metadata and signature events      | Per lease/legal requirements           | Lease and signature evidence retained for enforceability and dispute defense. |

## 3. Deletion and minimization

- Scheduled retention jobs remove expired data categories where no hold applies.
- Storage is minimized to required operational and legal fields.
- Sensitive fields are tokenized or masked when full values are unnecessary.

## 4. Audit log export capability

Authorized managers/admins can export operational datasets (including visitor logs) and the associated export actions are audit logged.

This ensures compliance commitments include both:

- **Retention** of oversight evidence, and
- **Traceability** of who accessed/exported regulated records.

## 5. Region-specific handling

Retention periods in this policy are baseline defaults. If local law requires stricter retention, shorter limits, or additional notice obligations, property-specific configuration and procedures must be applied.

## 6. Requests and exceptions

Data-subject requests, legal holds, and regulator requests may override standard deletion timelines. Requests should flow through property management and designated support/compliance channels.
